Rhetorical situation, identification, symbolic action, ideographs, rhetorical criticism, constitutive rhetoric, vernacular rhetoric, visual rhetoric, digital rhetoric, rhetorical agency, cultural criticism

- The strategic and contextual nature of rhetorical choices and their cultural effects
- The relationship between rhetorical form and social and political function

- Treating rhetoric as mere decoration or manipulation independent of substantive content
