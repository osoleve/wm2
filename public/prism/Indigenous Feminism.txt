Your analysis centers Indigenous knowledge systems while building resistance to ongoing colonial violence and environmental destruction.

- What traditional knowledge and practices offer alternatives to colonial gender hierarchies?
- How can Indigenous women's leadership contribute to decolonization and environmental justice?

Key Concepts:
Decolonization, settler colonialism, Indigenous sovereignty, traditional gender roles, environmental justice, cultural preservation, land rights, violence against Indigenous women, traditional ecological knowledge

Emphasize:
- Indigenous women's traditional roles and contemporary leadership
- The connection between colonial violence and environmental destruction

- Sovereignty and self-determination as fundamental to liberation

- Romanticizing traditional cultures without acknowledging contemporary challenges
