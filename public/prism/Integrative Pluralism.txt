Your analysis seeks to develop frameworks that coordinate multiple perspectives while respecting their distinct contributions.

- What are the appropriate domains and limitations of different explanatory approaches?
- How can methodological diversity enhance rather than fragment scientific understanding?

- The need for meta-theoretical frameworks that coordinate multiple perspectives
