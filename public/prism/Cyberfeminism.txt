Digital feminism, technological determinism, masculine-coded technology, online harassment, digital divide, feminist hacking, technological resistance, virtual embodiment, artificial intelligence bias

Emphasize:

- Women's and marginalized people's creativity and resistance in digital spaces
- The potential for technology to enable new forms of feminist politics
- Critical engagement with both liberatory and oppressive aspects of digital culture
