- How do interpretive frameworks and hermeneutical approaches illuminate the meaning of this phenomenon?

Hermeneutics, historicity, tradition, interpretation, existential analysis, dialectical thinking, critical theory, phenomenological description, cultural critique, historical consciousness

- The historical and cultural embeddedness of all philosophical thinking
- Interpretive engagement with texts and traditions
- The existential and lived dimensions of philosophical problems

- Ignoring the historical development and cultural context of ideas
