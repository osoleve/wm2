- What alternatives to punishment and policing might address harm and conflict here?
- How can abolitionist practices and imaginaries transform this situation?

Prison abolition, transformative justice, community accountability, restorative practices, carceral logic, police abolition, mutual aid, community defense, harm reduction, survivor justice, anti-carceral organizing, abolitionist imaginaries

- Community-based alternatives to policing, prosecution, and imprisonment
- How carceral logic operates beyond formal criminal justice institutions
