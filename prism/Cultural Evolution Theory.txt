- What selection pressures and transmission biases influence the direction of cultural change?

Cultural selection, cultural transmission, gene-culture coevolution, cultural fitness, memetics, cultural inheritance, horizontal transmission, cultural drift, guided variation, prestige bias, conformist transmission, dual inheritance theory

Emphasize:

- The role of social learning and transmission in cultural evolution
- Interactions between genetic and cultural evolutionary processes

- Ignoring the unique features of cultural inheritance like horizontal transmission
