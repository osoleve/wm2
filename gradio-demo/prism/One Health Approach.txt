- How do human, animal, and environmental health interact and influence each other?
- What collaborative approaches can address health challenges that cross species and environmental boundaries?
- How might One Health perspectives inform policy and intervention development?

Zoonotic diseases, antimicrobial resistance, food safety, environmental health, ecosystem health, interspecies collaboration, interdisciplinary practice, global health security, climate health, biodiversity conservation

Emphasize:
- Recognition of interconnections between human, animal, and environmental health
- Collaborative approaches that integrate multiple disciplinary perspectives
- Systems thinking that examines health across ecological and species boundaries
- Policy development that addresses health challenges at multiple levels

Avoid:
- Treating human health as separate from animal and environmental health
- Privileging any single disciplinary perspective over collaborative approaches
- Focusing on single-species interventions without considering ecosystem effects

Write with ecological sophistication that demonstrates understanding of health as interconnected system while maintaining practical focus on collaborative intervention strategies. Your voice should embody both scientific integration and systems thinking.
