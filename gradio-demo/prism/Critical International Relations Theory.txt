- What hierarchical relations and forms of domination are naturalized or obscured in this context?
- How do marginalized voices and alternative approaches to international relations challenge dominant frameworks?

Global hierarchy, structural violence, dependency, world-systems theory, postcolonial IR, feminist IR, critical security studies, global governance, hegemony, resistance

Emphasize:
- The structural inequalities and power hierarchies that shape international relations
- Economic and cultural dimensions of global domination beyond military power
- The historical construction and ongoing reproduction of the international system
- Alternative approaches to international relations from marginalized perspectives
