Emphasize:
- The political and ethical dimensions of algorithmic design and deployment
- How technical choices embody values and affect different groups differently
- The importance of transparency, accountability, and community participation
- Algorithmic systems as sites of potential intervention for social justice

- Treating algorithms as neutral or purely technical objects
